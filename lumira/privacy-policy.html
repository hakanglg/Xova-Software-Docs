Privacy Policy

How Lumira (by Hakan GOLGE) handles your data and privacy.

1. Overview

This privacy policy applies to the Lumira app (the "Application"), an AI-powered spoken-English practice app, created by Hakan GOLGE ("Service Provider"). Lumira lets you have real-time voice conversations with an AI tutor persona to practice speaking English. This policy explains what data the Application collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

The Application collects the following categories of information:

  • Account information — email address and display name, when you sign in with Apple, Google, or email.
  • Purchase history — your subscription status and purchase history, managed via RevenueCat, to unlock premium features.
  • Conversation content — text transcripts of your practice conversations with the AI tutor, stored in your account so your lesson and chat history can sync across sessions.
  • Voice audio — while you are speaking with the AI tutor, your microphone audio is streamed in real time to OpenAI's Realtime API to generate the AI's spoken responses. Lumira does not store your voice recordings on its own servers. Audio is processed transiently and is not retained or linked to your account by us.
  • Usage & analytics data — app interactions such as lessons completed, paywall views, and feature usage, collected via Firebase Analytics.
  • Diagnostic data — anonymous crash and performance data, collected via Firebase Crashlytics, to help us fix bugs.

The Application does not collect precise location data, contacts, photos, health data, or financial/payment card details — all payments are handled directly by the Apple App Store.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above solely to operate and improve the Application:

  • To authenticate you and sync your progress, saved tutor persona, and conversation history across devices.
  • To generate the AI tutor's real-time spoken and written responses during a practice conversation.
  • To manage your subscription entitlements and restore purchases.
  • To understand feature usage in aggregate and improve the product.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and technical issues.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising or cross-app/cross-site tracking.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Cloud Functions) — Google
  • OpenAI (Realtime voice API — powers the AI tutor's speech)
  • RevenueCat (subscription management)
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases)
  • Google (Google Sign-In)

We may also disclose your information:

  • As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process
  • To protect rights, safety, investigate fraud, or respond to a lawful government request
  • With trusted service providers listed above, solely to operate the Application

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account data and conversation transcripts for as long as you keep your account, so your progress and history remain available to you. Voice audio itself is not retained by us — it is streamed for real-time processing only.

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting hakanglge1@gmail.com. We will delete your data within a reasonable time, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law, KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact hakanglge1@gmail.com.

7. Children

Lumira is intended for adult learners (17+) and is not directed at children. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact hakanglge1@gmail.com so it can be removed.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — including encrypted network connections (HTTPS/WSS) and access-controlled cloud infrastructure (Firebase) — to protect the information processed and stored by the Application. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information to industry standards.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the Application after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact the Service Provider at hakanglge1@gmail.com.
