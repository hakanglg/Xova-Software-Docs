Frequently Asked Questions

How does speaking practice with the AI tutor work?

Pick an AI tutor persona, tap to talk, and have a real-time voice conversation in English. You can follow a structured lesson (Greetings, Introductions, Family, and more) or start a free-form scenario like ordering coffee or practicing for a job interview.

What's included in the free tier?

Every account gets 10 minutes of free voice practice per day with the Lumira persona. Premium unlocks unlimited speaking time and all 6 AI tutor personas.

Is my voice recorded or stored?

No. Your microphone audio is streamed for real-time processing only and is not stored by Lumira. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through the App Store. On your iPhone, go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions to cancel, upgrade, or view billing. See our Terms & Conditions for details on auto-renewal.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Voice conversations are streamed in real time, so an active Wi-Fi or mobile data connection is required.
